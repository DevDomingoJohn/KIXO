FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

FIND QUICK ANSWERS.

Browse our most commonly asked questions about shipping, returns, warranty, payments, and more.

How long will my order arrive?

Standard delivery takes 5–9 business days, while Express Delivery takes 2–4 business days in selected areas.

Is there free shipping?

Yes! Orders worth ₱10,500 and above qualify for free standard delivery.

Can I change my delivery address?

Yes, but only before your order has been shipped. Contact us immediately with your order number.

What is your return policy?

Items may be returned or exchanged within 7 days from the delivery date, provided they are unworn, with original tags and packaging intact.

How do I start a return or exchange?

Contact our support team with your order number and reason for return. Return shipping costs are shouldered by the customer unless the item received is incorrect or defective.

When will I get my refund?

Refunds are processed within 3–5 business days after we receive and inspect the returned item.

Can I exchange my shoes for a different size?

Yes. Size exchanges are available within 7 days, provided the shoes are unworn and still in their original condition.

Do your shoes come with a warranty?

Yes. All KIXO footwear includes a 30-day warranty against manufacturing defects such as sole separation, stitching failure, or material defects.

What if I received a defective or wrong item?

Contact us within 48 hours of delivery and send photos of the item, packaging, and shipping label. We will replace it or issue a full refund at no additional cost.

How do I know if my item is covered by warranty?

Keep your order receipt or confirmation email. Send us photos of the issue and our support team will assess your warranty claim promptly.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept GCash, Maya, major credit and debit cards, bank transfers, and Cash on Delivery (selected areas only).

Can I cancel my order?

Orders may be cancelled before they are packed or shipped. Contact our support team as soon as possible with your order number.

How can I track my order?

Once your order has been shipped, you will receive a tracking number through your registered email.

Do I need an account to place an order?

No. You may checkout as a guest, although creating an account lets you view your order history and receive updates faster.
